Annual Resource Report 2025

Published by the DocVault Editorial Team · Last updated March 2025

This report presents an overview of the year's most significant developments, data trends, and community-driven insights. It is intended as a free, accessible reference document for anyone who needs a clear and organized summary of the year's key findings.

All data referenced in this document has been compiled from publicly available sources. Where estimates are used, methodology notes are included in the appendix available in the downloadable PDF version.

Overview & Key Findings

The past year saw a marked increase in demand for plain-language, accessible documentation across all categories. The most-accessed resources were introductory guides and how-to documents — reflecting a growing need for entry-level reference material that is free from jargon.

"The single most impactful thing an organization can do is make its knowledge freely and clearly available. Everything else builds on that foundation."

Documents in the Research & Reports category saw the highest average read time (11 minutes), while How-To Guides had the highest download volume overall. This suggests users approach these two categories with different intentions — deep reading vs. quick reference.

Trends by Category

Getting Started

Introductory documents remained the most-visited section, with new visitors accounting for 68% of traffic. The Welcome Guide alone was viewed over 2,400 times since its publication in February.

Research & Reports

Six new reports were published this year, covering a range of topics including community impact, industry analysis, and a five-year comparative overview. These documents average 3.2 MB in file size and are among the most shared via external links.

  • Q3 Industry Analysis — most downloaded single document (1,840 downloads)
  • Community Impact Study — highest external share rate
  • 5-Year Comparative Overview — longest average reading time

How-To Guides

The how-to section grew from 4 to 8 documents this year. The addition of HTML read-online versions for three of these guides contributed to a 40% increase in engagement — confirming that in-browser reading reduces friction significantly for casual users.

Recommendations for 2026

Based on usage data and user feedback collected via the contact form, the following priorities are recommended for the coming year:

  • Convert the remaining 10 PDF-only documents to HTML read-online format
  • Add a search-within-document feature for longer reports
  • Introduce a printable one-page summary for each report
  • Expand the Forms & Templates category based on user requests

Conclusion

This library exists to make good information accessible without barriers. The data from 2025 confirms that free, well-organized, clearly written documents are consistently valuable — regardless of topic or format.

The full dataset, methodology notes, and appendix are available in the downloadable PDF version of this report. All documents on this site remain free to read, download, and share.
